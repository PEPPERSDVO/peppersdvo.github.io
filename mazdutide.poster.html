What is Mazdutide?

Mazdutide is a once-weekly dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist modeled on the gut hormone oxyntomodulin. China approved it for weight management in June 2025, the first drug of its class approved anywhere.

Strongest evidence: A · Approved use

Reconstitution

Add 1 mL bacteriostatic water to the 10 mg vial.

This yields 10 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 1 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Weight loss: −14.84% at 6 mg over 48 weeks (GLORY-1). B · Human trials
  • Approved: NMPA approval for weight management, June 2025. A · Approved use
  • Glucagon arm: Proposed energy-expenditure effect (oxyntomodulin concept). B · Human trials
  • Appetite: GLP-1R satiety + slower gastric emptying. B · Human trials

Potential side effects

  • GI effects are expected (GLP-1 class)
  • Rates weren't pulled from GLORY-1 this wave

The catalog's 4.5 mg step isn't a trial dose (GLORY-1 used 4 and 6 mg).
