What is Adipotide?

Adipotide is an experimental peptide that homes to the blood vessels feeding fat tissue and kills those vessel cells, so the fat loses its blood supply. Obese monkeys lost about 11% of body weight in 4 weeks, with reversible kidney toxicity. No human data have been published.

Strongest evidence: D · Animal / lab

Reconstitution

Add 2.5 mL bacteriostatic water to the 10 mg vial.

This yields 4 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2.5 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Primate weight loss: ≈11% in 28 days in obese rhesus monkeys. D · Animal / lab
  • Abdominal fat: −27% abdominal fat in monkeys. D · Animal / lab
  • Kidneys: Dose-dependent, reversible kidney toxicity. D · Animal / lab
  • Human data: None published. N/E · Not enough evidence

Potential side effects

  • Kidney toxicity (primates, reversible)
  • Dehydration and reduced food intake in primates
  • No human data
