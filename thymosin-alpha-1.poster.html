Thymic peptide approved abroad for hepatitis B

What is Thymosin Alpha-1?

Thymosin alpha-1 (thymalfasin) is a natural thymus peptide that strengthens T-cell immunity. As Zadaxin it's approved in about 35 countries for chronic hepatitis B, at 1.6 mg twice a week. It isn't approved in the US or EU.

Strongest evidence: A · Approved use

Reconstitution

Add 2 mL bacteriostatic water to the 10 mg vial.

This yields 5 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.
