What is Crystagen?

Crystagen is one of the short 'bioregulator' peptides from Vladimir Khavinson's group in Russia, claimed to normalize thymus / immune function. The research comes almost entirely from that one group, and no independent human trial of Crystagen was found.

Strongest evidence: D · Animal / lab

Reconstitution

Add 2 mL bacteriostatic water to the 20 mg vial.

This yields 10 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.
