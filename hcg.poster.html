What is hCG?

hCG is a hormone that acts like LH, the pituitary signal that tells the testes to make testosterone. It's FDA-approved for male hypogonadism and fertility treatment. It's widely used alongside testosterone therapy to keep the testes working. Its label explicitly says it doesn't help with weight loss.

Strongest evidence: A · Approved use

Reconstitution

Add 2 mL bacteriostatic water to the 5000 IU vial.

This yields 2500 IU/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Approved: Hypogonadism, cryptorchidism, ovulation induction. A · Approved use
  • On TRT: 250 IU every other day kept intratesticular T normal. B · Human trials
  • Not for weight loss: The label says hCG doesn't add weight loss. A · Approved use
  • Estrogen: Gynecomastia and edema are listed reactions. A · Approved use

Potential side effects

  • Headache, irritability, fatigue
  • Edema; gynecomastia
  • Injection-site pain
  • Women: ovarian hyperstimulation
