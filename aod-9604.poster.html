What is AOD-9604?

AOD-9604 is a 16-amino-acid fragment of human growth hormone. It was designed to keep a claimed fat-burning effect without GH's growth or blood-sugar effects. It proved safe in human trials, but its 24-week phase 2b found no weight loss beyond placebo.

Strongest evidence: B · Human trials

Reconstitution

Add 2.5 mL bacteriostatic water to the 5 mg vial.

This yields 2 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2.5 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

  • Status: Not approved; TGA rejected the obesity indication. B · Human trials
