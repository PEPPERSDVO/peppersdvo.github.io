What is Cerebrolysin?

Cerebrolysin is a peptide mixture made from pig brain. It's sold as an IV solution in many countries outside the US for dementia, stroke and brain injury. A Cochrane review found no survival benefit in stroke and more non-fatal serious adverse events. The catalog's powder, dosed in milligrams, isn't the same product.

Strongest evidence: A · Approved use

Reconstitution

Add 8 mL bacteriostatic water to the 64 mg vial.

This yields 8 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 8 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Approved abroad: IV solution for dementia, stroke, brain trauma. A · Approved use
  • Stroke review: No mortality benefit; more non-fatal serious AEs. A · Approved use
  • Catalog form: Powder at mg doses vs a 215 mg/mL IV solution. E · Community / vendor

Potential side effects

  • Non-fatal serious adverse events increased in stroke trials
  • Contraindicated in epilepsy

Form: the approved Cerebrolysin is a 215.2 mg/mL solution. The catalog sells a powder at about 8 mg/mL after mixing.

Dose: clinical use is milliliters IV (e.g. 30 mL/day); the catalog's 8–24 mg/day SC is a tiny fraction.

For subcutaneous injection
Refrigerate after mixing. Catalog: use within 60 days.
