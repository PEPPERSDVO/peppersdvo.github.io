Intranasal form: does it get in?

What is it?

Bremelanotide / PT-141 (nasal) is the catalog's intranasal version of PT-141. Intranasal bremelanotide WAS tested: the FDA halted the phase II nasal trials in 2007 because of blood-pressure increases, and the nasal form was abandoned in 2008. The developer blamed erratic nasal uptake causing high blood levels in some users; it was reformulated as the SC injection now approved.
