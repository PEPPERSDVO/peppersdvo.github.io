The body's master antioxidant

What is Glutathione?

Glutathione is a three-amino-acid antioxidant every cell makes for itself. IV glutathione was safe in a small Parkinson's trial, but its benefit wasn't significant. No study has looked at small under-the-skin doses.

Strongest evidence: B · Human trials

Reconstitution

Add 7 mL bacteriostatic water to the 1500 mg vial.

This yields 214.3 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 7 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Antioxidant: The main intracellular redox buffer. B · Human trials

Potential side effects

  • Well tolerated IV in the Parkinson's pilot
  • Injection-site irritation
  • Rare hypersensitivity
