IGF-1 LR3 1mg

QUICK GUIDE

Long-acting IGF-1 analog, no human data

What is IGF-1 LR3?

IGF-1 LR3 is an engineered, longer-lasting form of insulin-like growth factor 1, made mainly as a cell-culture reagent. It acts directly on IGF-1 receptors and bypasses growth hormone. It has never been studied in humans. Its approved cousin, mecasermin, carries a serious hypoglycemia warning.

Strongest evidence: D · Animal / lab

Reconstitution

Add 2.5 mL 0.6% acetic acid to the 1 mg vial.

This yields 0.4 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2.5 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • Direct IGF-1: Bypasses GH; weak binding to IGF-binding proteins. D · Animal / lab
  • Hypoglycemia: The approved IGF-1 drug warns of severe hypoglycemia. A · Approved use
  • Human data: None. N/E · Not enough evidence

Potential side effects

  • Hypoglycemia (class)
  • No human data for LR3

Catalog advises fasted dosing. The approved IGF-1 drug's label says to dose with a meal to avoid hypoglycemia.
