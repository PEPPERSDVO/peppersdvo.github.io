What is VIP?

VIP is a natural neuropeptide that relaxes blood vessels and airways and calms inflammation. Its synthetic version, aviptadil, is approved in the UK with phentolamine as an erectile-dysfunction injection. IV aviptadil failed in COVID trials, and daily under-the-skin use has no data.

Strongest evidence: A · Approved use

Reconstitution

Add 2.5 mL bacteriostatic water to the 10 mg vial.

This yields 4 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2.5 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.

Potential benefits

  • ED (abroad): Invicorp: aviptadil + phentolamine, UK 2015. A · Approved use
  • COVID trials: Primary endpoint missed; NIH trial stopped for futility. B · Human trials
  • SC use: No human data. N/E · Not enough evidence

Potential side effects

  • Flushing, low blood pressure (vasodilation)
  • Diarrhea
  • No SC human data
