What is LIPO-C+?

LIPO-C+ is a pre-mixed "lipotropic" injection (l-carnitine, methionine, choline, inositol, lidocaine, vitamin b6; benzyl alcohol preservative). These nutrients help the liver move fat, but no randomized trial shows the injections cause weight loss beyond diet. The catalog doesn't state how much of each ingredient is in the vial.

Strongest evidence: C · Human observational

Preparation

Pre-made solution: no mixing.

  1. Swab the vial top with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw the dose straight from the vial.
  3. Refrigerate; follow the vial label.

Potential benefits

  • Liver fat role: Choline etc. support fat export; not proof of fat loss. C · Human observational
  • Concentrations: Not stated: the per-ingredient dose is unknown. E · Community / vendor
  • Trials: No RCTs of MIC injections for weight loss. C · Human observational

Potential side effects

  • Injection-site pain
  • Ingredient-specific effects (unknown amounts)

The catalog writes doses as "50–100 units (500–1,000 mcg)" without saying which ingredient the mcg refers to.

Vial size and per-ingredient concentrations aren't stated.
