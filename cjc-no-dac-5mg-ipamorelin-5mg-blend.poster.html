What is it?

CJC-1295 (no DAC) / Ipamorelin 5/5 is a pre-made catalog blend of CJC-1295 (no DAC), Ipamorelin. Each ingredient keeps its own evidence grade (CJC-1295 (no DAC) E, Ipamorelin B). No study of this combination was found.

Strongest evidence: E · Community / vendor

Reconstitution

Add 2.5 mL bacteriostatic water to the 10 mg vial.

This yields 4 mg/mL.
  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad.
  2. Draw 2.5 mL of water and inject it slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Roll gently until dissolved; don't shake.
  4. Label and refrigerate.
